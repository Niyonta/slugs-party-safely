🛡️1 — Definitions and Policies

Definitions - UC Sexual Violence and Sexual Harassment (SVSH) Policy
Consent
Affirmative, conscious, and voluntary - revocable at any time.
Affirmative Conscious Voluntary Revocable
Affirmative also requires:
Conscious Freely given Mutual
⚡ Incapacitation
Beyond drunkenness or intoxication. Not automatic from drinking - but substances are always a warning sign.
⚡ Sexual Violence
Sexual assault (contact or penetration), relationship violence, stalking, and sexual exploitation. Always requires absence of consent.
🗣️ Sexual Harassment
Unwelcome sexual conduct sufficiently severe or pervasive to interfere with a person's ability to participate in education or employment.
🛡️ Amnesty Policy
Alcohol or drug use will not result in a conduct violation for a student who reports. (UCSC RAP)
Under SVSH Policy - none of the following constitute consent:
✗ Silence ✗ Pressure or coercion ✗ Past consent ✗ Drug- or alcohol-facilitated ✗ Incapacitation

⚠️3 — Alcohol and Drugs

Substances affect everyone in the room - including your ability to give and obtain consent, and your legal and ethical responsibilities.

BAC impairment scale - drag to explore
Key: 1 standard drink = 12 oz regular beer (5%) = 5 oz wine (12%) = 1.5 oz spirits (40%). Each raises BAC by approximately 0.02-0.03%.
Move the slider to explore impairment levels.
✗
"Drunk but conscious = can consent."
Intoxication compromises judgment and communication. Initiating with someone visibly impaired puts you at serious legal and ethical risk - even if they don't say "no"
✗
"I was drunk too, so I'm not responsible."
Under UC SVSH Policy, being intoxicated does not remove your obligation to obtain consent. You can be found responsible even while under the influence.

🆘6 — Recognizing and Reporting Incidents

Partying safely also means knowing what to do after.

Recognize it
- Unwanted touching or penetration
- Coercion or pressure
- Drug- or alcohol-facilitated contact
You don't need certainty to seek support.
Report it
- Online, by phone, or in person
- Can be filed by affected party or third party
- No decision on next steps required to report
Formal Investigation
- Administrative process
- Examines allegations, interviews parties
- Determines policy violation
- Not a criminal process
Alternative Resolution
- Voluntary and binding
- Both parties agree on terms
- Focused on remedy, not discipline
- No formal policy determination
🛡️ Amnesty
- Alcohol/drug use will not count against you
- Retaliation for reporting is a policy violation
- Anonymous: ucsc.edu/safety
Safety and Supportive Measures
- Academic adjustments
- Housing or schedule changes
- No-contact directives
- Available regardless of formal process

🤝5 — Practical Safety Toolkit

Before, during, and after.

✅ Before you go
Set limits with a trusted friend · Agree on a check-in signal · Know the address and a way home · Eat before drinking
👀 While you're there
Watch your drink · Check in on impaired friends · Trust your gut · "Are you okay?" is always right
🚨 If something feels off
You can always leave · A bad feeling is a reason · Bystander intervention saves lives · Call 911 if needed
